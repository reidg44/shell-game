# shell-game

## Platform

This App runs on the household Platform as `pennyhunt` (https://pennyhunt.apps.geyers.net). Read [`docs/platform.md`](docs/platform.md) before you change `main.tf`, `justfile`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` or `.copier-answers.yml`. The all-apps template owns those files: take changes with `copier update`, and put the App's own recipes in `app.just`.
